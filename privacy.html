Privacy Policy – Pinterest Eagle Importer

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Pinterest Eagle Importer is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jason Marder.

What it does. With my authorization, it reads my own Pinterest boards, board sections, and saved pins through the Pinterest API (read-only) and saves copies to my local Eagle image library on my own computer.

Data collection. It does not collect data from any other Pinterest user. It does not post, edit, or delete anything on Pinterest.

Storage and sharing. All data stays on my local computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Access tokens. OAuth tokens are stored locally in a private file and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest account settings under Apps.
